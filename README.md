Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second"** — one sentence, no more than 200 characters, pitching a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke, yet specific enough that a reader can see the customers and the money.

## The pitch

> We install vending machines in laundromats that sell single socks, because every lost sock is a customer already standing at the machine, and a sock costing us 40 cents sells for four dollars.

192 characters. One sentence. The odd thing being sold (single socks, from a vending machine), who pays (laundromat customers who just lost one), and why the money works (a 40-cent sock sells for four dollars) — all in the line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the pitch above and nothing else. Standard library only, no dependencies.

## Why it fits the brief

- **Genuinely bizarre:** a vending machine that sells one sock at a time is ridiculous on its face — nobody sets out to buy a single sock.
- **Real on second look:** socks vanish in laundromats constantly, and the victim is already standing in front of the machine, wallet in hand, with an immediate need. The demand is created at the exact point of sale.
- **The money is visible:** 40 cents of cost against a four-dollar price is a 10x markup on a product whose customer needs it right now, and every machine sits where the problem happens daily.
